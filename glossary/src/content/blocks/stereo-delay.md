Stereo Delay is a [post-effect](/blocks/categories/effect/). When connected to a Source block, it creates an adjustable echo of the incoming audio, which bounces back and forth between the left and right stereo channels.

In the options, ‘Delay Time’ sets how soon the first echo is heard. ‘Feedback’ sends more of the signal back into the delay for a longer trail, and ‘Mix’ sets how much of the echo you hear. Turn on ‘Tempo Sync’ and choose a note length in ‘Delay’ to keep the echoes in time with the patch.

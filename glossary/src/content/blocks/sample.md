Sample is a [Source](/blocks/categories/source/) block. It loads an audio file from [Freesound](https://freesound.org/) or from your own device. Connect it to [Power](/blocks/power/) to make a loop, or connect a [Computer Keyboard](/blocks/computer-keyboard/) or [MIDI Controller](/blocks/midi-controller/) to play the same sound at different pitches.

In its options, ‘Load New Sample’ opens the sample browser. You can search Freesound, try a random search, or choose ‘Your File’. You can also drop an audio file onto the Sample block.

‘Region’ shows the waveform. Drag the start and end markers to choose the section you want to play. ‘Loop’ reveals the loop markers for the section that repeats. ‘Reverse’ flips the waveform and its markers, and ‘Playback’ changes the speed and pitch. ‘Detune’ changes the pitch in semitone steps.

Try taking a short section of a drum loop and playing it back in reverse. If you share a patch that uses a file from your device, the patch keeps a reference to the file. The person opening it will need to supply the audio.

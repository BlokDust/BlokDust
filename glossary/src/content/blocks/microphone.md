Microphone is a [Source](/blocks/categories/source/) block. It allows you to use your computer/device’s inbuilt microphone as a sound source. Alternatively, if you have other audio inputs connected such as a USB microphone, or an audio interface, those can be used as your sound source.

This lets you run your voice through any of BlokDust’s [Effects](/blocks/categories/effect/), and with an audio interface, other instruments such as an electric guitar can be used.

On the website, allow microphone access when your browser asks. If you open someone else’s patch, its microphone starts off until you choose ‘Turn On’. Select an input in your browser or device settings, then hold the block or connect it to a [Power](/blocks/power/) block to hear it. ‘Gain’ sets the input level. Use headphones to avoid feedback.

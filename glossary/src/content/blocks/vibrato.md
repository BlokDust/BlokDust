Vibrato is a [pre-effect](/blocks/categories/effect/). It affects the sound of a connected [Source](/blocks/categories/source/) block by quickly altering its pitch up and down slightly. This kind of modulation creates variety in the sound, and can range from a “wobble”, to a more gradual “wow”, or to siren, alarm, or ringing type sounds.

Vibrato uses an [LFO](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Low-frequency_oscillation) to modulate the pitch.

In its options, ‘Rate’ sets the speed of the modulation, ‘Depth’ sets the amount of pitch variation, and ‘Wave’ sets the waveform shape which the effect follows (Sine, Square, Triangle, Saw).

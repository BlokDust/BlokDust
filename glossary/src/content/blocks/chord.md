Chord is a [note effect](/blocks/categories/effect/). When a connected [Source](/blocks/categories/source/) plays a note, the Chord block can add other notes above it. A different chord can be set for each note from C through B.

In the options, drag a bar in ‘Chords’ to choose its chord. The bottom setting plays the original note on its own. ‘Key’ shifts the whole set of chords round the 12 notes. ‘Smooth’ brings the upper notes closer to their previous positions so that the change from one chord to another is less jumpy.

Try connecting Chord and a [Computer Keyboard](/blocks/computer-keyboard/) to a [Tone](/blocks/tone/). Play one key at a time and listen to how the chords change. Add an [Arpeggiator](/blocks/arpeggiator/) to hear the notes of each chord in turn.

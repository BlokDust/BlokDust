Filter is a [post-effect](/blocks/categories/effect/). It shapes the audio of any connected [Source](/blocks/categories/source/) blocks by changing the level of selected frequencies. Low-pass lets the lows through, high-pass lets the highs through, band-pass keeps a band of frequencies, and peak boosts or cuts a band around a chosen frequency.

In the options, choose a filter type and drag the point on the graph to change its frequency. Drag it up or down to change the gain or resonance, depending on the type. In peak and band-pass modes, drag the width bar to change how wide a range of frequencies the filter affects.

Try a low-pass Filter on a sawtooth [Tone](/blocks/tone/). Start with the frequency high and bring it down to hear the buzzy harmonics disappear.

Touchpad is an [Interaction](/blocks/categories/interaction/) block. Move it near a [Source](/blocks/categories/source/), open its options and press the pads to play notes. On a touchscreen you can hold several pads at once in polyphonic mode. Slide from pad to pad to move between notes.

In the options, ‘Key’ sets the starting note and ‘Octave’ moves the whole grid up or down. With ‘In Key’ on, the pads follow the selected ‘Scale’. Switch it off to play all the semitones, and use ‘Mono/Poly’ to choose one voice or several.

Try a Touchpad with a [Wavetable](/blocks/wavetable/) and a little [Stereo Delay](/blocks/stereo-delay/). Set it to a pentatonic scale and slide across a row of pads.

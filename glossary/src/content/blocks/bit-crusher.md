Bit Crusher is a [post-effect](/blocks/categories/effect/). It processes the audio signal coming from any [Source](/blocks/categories/source/) blocks connected to it by reducing the bit depth, intentionally degrading the quality and creating distortion.

In the Bit Crusher’s options, setting the number of ‘bits’ defines the amount of reduction. The fewer bits, the stronger the effect, and the more simplified the incoming audio’s waveform becomes.

Bitcrushing is seen as a “lo-fi” effect, it embraces a quirk of how audio is digitally represented at low quality.

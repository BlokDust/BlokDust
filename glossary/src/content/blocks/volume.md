Volume is a [post-effect](/blocks/categories/effect/). It can reduce or boost the level of connected audio by -20 [decibels](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Decibel) to +20 decibels. In its options, ‘Level’ sets the amount the signal is boosted or reduced.

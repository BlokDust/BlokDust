Convolution is a [post-effect](/blocks/categories/effect/). In audio signal processing, convolution reverb is a process used for digitally simulating the reverberation of a space based on a pre-recorded audio sample or [impulse response](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Impulse_response).

In the options, open the sample browser to choose an impulse response from [Freesound](https://freesound.org/) or load your own file. The first 2.5 seconds are used. ‘Mix’ sets the balance between the original sound and the reverb.

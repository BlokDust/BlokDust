Stage In is an [Interaction](/blocks/categories/interaction/) block you’ll find inside a [Stage](/blocks/stage/). Connect it to a [Source](/blocks/categories/source/) in there, then connect a controller to the Stage block outside. The notes you play travel through the Stage In and play the inner source.

Stage In follows the settings of the controller outside. Set ‘Mono/Poly’, ‘Glide’ or ‘Silent Keys’ on that controller.

Try a [Computer Keyboard](/blocks/computer-keyboard/) beside the Stage outside, and a Stage In beside a [Tone](/blocks/tone/) inside. Add an [Envelope](/blocks/envelope/) inside to shape how the notes fade in and out.

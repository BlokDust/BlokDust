Scuzz is a [pre-effect](/blocks/categories/effect/). It directly modifies the pitch of any [Source](/blocks/categories/source/) blocks connected and rapidly modulates it via an [LFO](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Low-frequency_oscillation) set to higher than usual ranges.

The sound can vary from messy pulsing, to metallic sounding harmonics, a little like a [ring modulator](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ring_modulation).

In its options, ‘Power’ sets the depth of the effect, and ‘Pulverisation’ sets the rate, ranging from audible modulation, to where the rate becomes an audible frequency.

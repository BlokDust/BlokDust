Prism is a [Power](/blocks/categories/power/) block. Aim a [Laser](/blocks/laser/) or [Particle Emitter](/blocks/particle-emitter/) into it and the beam or particles will come out of the Prism’s centre in a fan. Each new path has the distance or time the original had left.

In the options, ‘Angle’ points the centre of the fan, ‘Streams’ sets how many paths come out, and ‘Spread’ sets how wide the fan is. ‘Rotate’ turns the whole fan. A single stream is handy for sending a beam round a corner.

Try three streams and put [Tone](/blocks/tone/) blocks in their paths. Slowly rotate the Prism to play the tones as the beams pass over them.

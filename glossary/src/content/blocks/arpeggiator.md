Arpeggiator is a [note effect](/blocks/categories/effect/). It takes the notes a [Source](/blocks/categories/source/) block is being asked to play, and plays them one after another. The notes can come from keys you’re holding down, or from a [Chord](/blocks/chord/) block connected to the same source.

In the options, ‘Pattern’ sets the order of the notes, and ‘Octaves’ sets how many octaves the pattern covers. ‘Speed’ sets how fast it steps. Turn on ‘Tempo Sync’ to set the speed in musical divisions, or choose ‘Per Hit’ to advance the pattern each time the source is triggered.

Try holding three notes on a [Computer Keyboard](/blocks/computer-keyboard/) and switching the pattern from ‘Up’ to ‘Up & Down’.

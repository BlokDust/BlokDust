Pulse Power is a [Power category](/blocks/categories/power/) block. When powered, it creates a pulse of power which it relays onto any connected [Source](/blocks/categories/source/) blocks, [Particle Emitters](/blocks/particle-emitter/) or [Laser](/blocks/laser/) blocks, triggering their audio (in the case of Source blocks) or causing them to momentarily emit particles or laser beams (in the case of Particle Emitters & Laser).

It becomes powered when it comes into contact with any particles fired from Particle Emitters, or any laser beams fired from Laser Blocks. It can also be powered manually.

In its options, ‘Pulse Length’ sets how long connected blocks stay powered. ‘Delay’ sets how long the Pulse waits after a hit before it powers them. Turn on ‘Tempo Sync’ to set both the length and the delay in beats. Use ‘Pulse On’ to try a pulse by hand.

Blocks in the Power category can be combined to create interesting reactions, and self-playing environments. Read more on this subject on the [Power page](/blocks/categories/power/).

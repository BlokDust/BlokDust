LFO is an [Effect](/blocks/categories/effect/) block. LFO stands for low-frequency oscillator. Here its wave moves a control, such as the frequency of a [Filter](/blocks/filter/) or the angle of a [Laser](/blocks/laser/). Move it near another block to see which of that block’s controls it can move.

In the options, ‘Target’ selects the block and setting. ‘Shape’ sets the shape of the movement, ‘Rate’ sets its speed and ‘Depth’ sets how far it moves. ‘Phase’ shifts the starting point in the cycle. Turn on ‘Tempo Sync’ to set the rate in musical divisions.

Try a slow triangle shape on a Filter’s frequency. Move the LFO away and the Filter returns to its own setting.

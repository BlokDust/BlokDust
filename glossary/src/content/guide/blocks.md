Blocks can be created by dragging & dropping from the top menu, and come in four categories: [Source](/blocks/categories/source/), [Effects](/blocks/categories/effect/), [Power](/blocks/categories/power/) & [Interaction](/blocks/categories/interaction/).

They will connect with other suitable blocks automatically when in close proximity, which is indicated with connection lines drawn between the blocks.

Source blocks create sound from a [sample](/blocks/sample/), an [oscillator](/blocks/tone/) or a [microphone input](/blocks/microphone/). Connect an Effect block to change that sound.

You can play a Source block by holding it, connecting power or an interaction block, or hitting it with a particle or laser. A [Get Started](/guide/get-started/) tutorial walks through a simple synth and a self-playing patch.

Each block has its own settings. Single-clicking on a block will open its options panel where various effect parameters can be changed or played.

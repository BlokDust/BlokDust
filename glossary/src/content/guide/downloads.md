## iPad

BlokDust for iPad includes the standalone app and an AUv3 plug-in for hosts such as AUM, GarageBand and Logic Pro. It requires iPadOS 16 or later and is coming to the App Store.

[Browse the App Store for iPad](https://apps.apple.com/us/ipad/apps).

You can also [open BlokDust](/) in your browser.

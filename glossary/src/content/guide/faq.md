## What can BlokDust do?

[BlokDust](/about/) is a music-making app. You can create playable synths, build sounds and soundscapes, make samplers, modify your voice, record yourself or set up self-playing contraptions. When you’ve made something cool, you can save a link and share it. What we really hope is that you’ll make new things and teach us what it can do.

## Do I need to be a musician?

Nope. There’s room to make complex things, and there’ll be things familiar to musicians, but anyone can have a go. What you make doesn’t have to be melodic. It could be a strange vocal effect, a soundscape or just something visual.

## What do I need to run it?

You can open BlokDust in a modern browser and start playing without an account. A desktop, laptop or tablet gives you room for more blocks. If you have a USB audio interface, try it with the [Microphone](/blocks/microphone/) block. If you have a [MIDI controller](/blocks/midi-controller/), plug it in too. There are also desktop and iPad apps and plug-ins.

## Can I use BlokDust sounds in my song?

Yes, we hope you do! You can record sounds you make and use them in your music. If you load a sound from [Freesound](https://freesound.org/), check that sound’s licence and follow any attribution or other conditions.

## Can I use my own sounds in BlokDust?

Yes. Open the options on a [Sample](/blocks/sample/), [Granular](/blocks/granular/) or [Convolution](/blocks/convolution/) block, open the sample browser and choose ‘Your File’. You can also drop an audio file onto the block. The patch saves a reference to your file. If someone else opens it, they’ll need to choose the audio file on their own device.

## Do I need an account to save or share?

You can make a patch, save a link and share it without signing in. Sign in to keep your patches together across devices and to publish them in the gallery.

## Is the source code available?

Yes. You can find [BlokDust 2 on GitHub](https://github.com/lukephills/blokdust-v2). The audio and simulation engine is written in C++. In the browser, it runs as WebAssembly with a TypeScript interface.

## Can I contribute?

Try publishing something in the gallery so others can pull it apart and learn from it. If you have a bug, an idea or a code contribution, you can use Feedback in Settings or visit the [GitHub project](https://github.com/lukephills/blokdust-v2).

## Can I use BlokDust as an offline app?

The standalone apps can run and open local patch files offline. Online features, such as searching Freesound and opening shared patches from the gallery, need an internet connection.

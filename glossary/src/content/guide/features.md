## Sound

- Make playable [synths](/guide/synths/) with [Tone](/blocks/tone/), [Wavetable](/blocks/wavetable/) or [Noise](/blocks/noise/).
- Load sounds from [Freesound](https://freesound.org/) or your own files. Play them with [Sample](/blocks/sample/), chop them into grains with [Granular](/blocks/granular/) or generate a sound from scratch with [SampleGen](/blocks/sample-gen/).
- Run a voice or instrument through [effects](/blocks/categories/effect/) with a [Microphone](/blocks/microphone/) in your browser or [Audio In](/blocks/audio-in/) in BlokDust FX. Capture the output with [Recorder](/blocks/recorder/) and download a WAV.

## Creation and interaction

- Drag blocks close together and they connect. Move them apart to break the connection.
- Build self-playing scenes with [Particle Emitters](/blocks/particle-emitter/), [Lasers](/blocks/laser/) and [power blocks](/blocks/categories/power/). Switch on Tempo Sync to set their timing in musical divisions.
- Play sources with a [Computer Keyboard](/blocks/computer-keyboard/), [MIDI Controller](/blocks/midi-controller/) or [Touchpad](/blocks/touchpad/). Add [Chord](/blocks/chord/), [Arpeggiator](/blocks/arpeggiator/) or [Random Note](/blocks/random-note/) to change the notes they play.
- Group a scene into a [Stage](/blocks/stage/), add Labels or change the colour theme in Settings.

## Saving and sharing

- Save a patch and share its link, export a patch file, or sign in to keep patches in your account.
- Publish a patch in the gallery, or open someone else’s and save your own remix.

## Where to play

Open BlokDust in your browser, or run it as a [standalone app or plug-in](/downloads/). Try the in-app tour in Settings or follow [Get Started](/guide/get-started/).

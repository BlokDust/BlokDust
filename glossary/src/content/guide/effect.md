Effect blocks can connect to a [Source](/blocks/categories/source/) block and alter that Source block’s sound. Some effects work inside the source and others process the audio it makes.

Pre-effects change a property of the Source block itself. [Envelope](/blocks/envelope/) shapes the way a note starts and ends. [Vibrato](/blocks/vibrato/) and [Scuzz](/blocks/scuzz/) change its pitch or playback rate.

Post-effects take the audio from a source and process it. Try [Filter](/blocks/filter/) to shape its tone, [Stereo Delay](/blocks/stereo-delay/) for an echo or [Compressor](/blocks/compressor/) to tame loud peaks. Note effects such as [Chord](/blocks/chord/) change which notes the source plays, and an [LFO](/blocks/lfo/) can move a setting on a nearby block.

Single-clicking on any Effect block will open up its options panel, where there are a range of individual settings to tailor that Effect.

Most Source blocks create sound when pressed, connected to [Power](/blocks/power/) or an [Interaction](/blocks/categories/interaction/) block, or hit by particles or a laser beam. Connect an [Effect](/blocks/categories/effect/) block to change the sound. [Audio In](/blocks/audio-in/) is an always-on source for the track running BlokDust FX.

The [Tone](/blocks/tone/) block is the most basic and allows for real synthesis in the browser, while the [Microphone](/blocks/microphone/) block not only lets you use your computer’s built-in microphone as a source (allowing you to add effects to your voice etc), but by using it in conjunction with an audio interface, any instrument can be plugged into BlokDust.

[Sample](/blocks/sample/) and [Granular](/blocks/granular/) let you load sounds from Freesound or your own device. [Wavetable](/blocks/wavetable/) makes tones that change shape, and a [Stage](/blocks/stage/) lets you use a whole group of blocks as one source.

[SampleGen](/blocks/sample-gen/) opens up further variety and originality by procedurally generating an original sample at the push of a button.

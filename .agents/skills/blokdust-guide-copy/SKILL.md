---
name: blokdust-guide-copy
description: Write or update BlokDust guide articles and block descriptions in the voice of the original guide.
---

- Open with what the block does in one or two sentences. Follow with “X is a [category] block” and explain what it connects to and what happens when it plays or is hit.
- Group controls into a short “In the options” paragraph. Use their on-screen names in single quotation marks and say what turning them up or down does.
- End with something small to try. Use concrete sounds and motions, like a hiss, wobble or beam, and link the blocks you mention.
- Keep the casual, matter-of-fact voice and British spelling of the [Noise](../../../glossary/src/content/blocks/noise.md) and [Stereo Delay](../../../glossary/src/content/blocks/stereo-delay.md) pages. Check names and behaviour against the current manifests and app before describing them.
